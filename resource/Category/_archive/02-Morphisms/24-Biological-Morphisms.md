## 1. Overview / 概述

**English / 英文**:

Biological morphisms represent biological processes, research transformations, and development operations. They capture biological transformations in biological research and project management. This document provides a category-theoretic perspective on biological morphisms, aligning with biological research standards.

**中文**:

生物态射表示生物过程、研究变换和开发操作。它们捕捉生物研究和项目管理中的生物变换。本文档从范畴论视角提供生物态射的定义，对齐生物研究标准。

**Key Insights / 关键洞察**:

- **Biological Processes / 生物过程**: Research, development, testing / 研究、开发、测试
- **Research Transformations / 研究变换**: Research process transformations / 研究过程变换
- **Development Operations / 开发操作**: Development process operations / 开发过程操作
- **Regulatory Compliance / 监管合规**: Regulatory compliance operations / 监管合规操作

## 2. Category Theory Definition / 范畴论定义

### 2.1 Biological Process Morphism / 生物过程态射

**Definition 2.1** (Biological Process Morphism)

A biological process morphism $process: P_1 \to P_2$:

$$process(P_1) = P_2$$

transforming biological projects.

### 2.2 Biological Properties / 生物性质

**Axiom 2.1** (Biological Safety)

Biological processes preserve safety:

$$\forall process: Safety(P_1) \Rightarrow Safety(P_2)$$
