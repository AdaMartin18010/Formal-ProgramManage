## 2. Definition / 定义

### 2.1 PMBOK 7th Edition Definition / PMBOK 第7版定义

**Definition 2.1** (Quality Assurance - PMBOK 7th Edition)

Quality assurance audits quality requirements and the results from quality control measurements to ensure appropriate quality standards and operational definitions are used.

**Key Activities / 关键活动**:

- **Process Auditing / 过程审计**: Auditing quality processes
- **Standard Compliance / 标准合规**: Ensuring standard compliance
- **Measurement Review / 测量审查**: Reviewing quality measurements
- **Improvement Actions / 改进行动**: Taking improvement actions

### 2.2 ISO 9001 Standard Definition / ISO 9001 标准定义

**Definition 2.2** (Quality Assurance - ISO 9001:2015)

Quality assurance provides confidence that quality requirements will be fulfilled.

### 2.3 Formal Mathematical Definition / 形式化数学定义

**Definition 2.3** (Quality Assurance - Formal Definition)

Quality assurance is a function:

$$assure: \mathbf{QualityPlan} \to \mathbf{QualityAssurance}$$

ensuring quality standards.

## 3. Category Theory Perspective / 范畴论视角

**Definition 3.1** (Quality Assurance Morphism)

A quality assurance morphism $assure: \mathbf{QualityPlan} \to \mathbf{QualityAssurance}$:

$$assure(QualityPlan) = QualityAssurance$$

### 3.2 Assurance Properties / 保证性质

**Property 3.1** (Assurance Effectiveness)

Quality assurance is effective:

$$assure(QualityPlan) \Rightarrow \text{quality standards met}$$

## 7. Explanations / 解释

### 7.1 Intuitive Explanation / 直观解释

**Explanation 7.1** ("Inspection" Metaphor)

Quality assurance is like inspection:

- **Standards / 标准**: Inspection criteria
- **Process / 过程**: Inspection process
- **Compliance / 合规**: Meeting standards
- **Improvement / 改进**: Continuous improvement

### 7.3 直观解释配例 / Intuitive Explanation with One Example

**Example 7.3** (质量保证即检验 + 一例 / Assurance as Inspection with One Example)

用「检验」比喻：标准=检验准则、过程=审计流程、合规=符合性、改进=持续改进。例如 $assure(QualityPlan_{dev})=QualityAssurance_{dev}$ 对开发过程做审计；$assure(QualityPlan_{iso})=QualityAssurance_{iso}$ 做 ISO 符合性核查。

## 8. Argumentation / 论证

### 8.1 Why Assurance Exists / 为什么保证存在

**Argument 8.1** (Confidence Argument)

Assurance exists because:

1. Quality needs confidence
2. Confidence requires assurance
3. Assurance provides verification
4. Verification enables trust
