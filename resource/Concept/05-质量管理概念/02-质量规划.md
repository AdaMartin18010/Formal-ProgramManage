## 2. Definition / 定义

### 2.1 PMBOK 7th Edition Definition / PMBOK 第7版定义

**Definition 2.1** (Quality Planning - PMBOK 7th Edition)

Quality planning identifies quality requirements and standards for the project and its deliverables, and documents how the project will demonstrate compliance.

**Key Activities / 关键活动**:

- **Requirement Identification / 需求识别**: Identifying quality requirements
- **Standard Selection / 标准选择**: Selecting quality standards
- **Metric Definition / 指标定义**: Defining quality metrics
- **Compliance Planning / 合规规划**: Planning compliance approach

### 2.2 ISO/IEC 25010 Standard Definition / ISO/IEC 25010 标准定义

**Definition 2.2** (Quality Planning - ISO/IEC 25010:2011)

Quality planning identifies quality characteristics and their levels.

### 2.3 Formal Mathematical Definition / 形式化数学定义

**Definition 2.3** (Quality Planning - Formal Definition)

Quality planning is a function:

$$plan: \mathbf{Project} \to \mathbf{QualityPlan}$$

where $QualityPlan = (Requirements, Standards, Metrics, Compliance)$.

## 3. Category Theory Perspective / 范畴论视角

### 3.1 Planning as Morphism / 规划作为态射

**Definition 3.1** (Quality Planning Morphism)

A quality planning morphism $plan: \mathbf{Project} \to \mathbf{QualityPlan}$:

$$plan(P) = QualityPlan$$

### 3.2 Planning Properties / 规划性质

**Property 3.1** (Planning Completeness)

Quality planning is complete:

$$plan(P) = QualityPlan \Rightarrow Complete(QualityPlan)$$

## 7. Explanations / 解释

### 7.1 Intuitive Explanation / 直观解释

**Explanation 7.1** ("Blueprint" Metaphor)

Quality planning is like a blueprint:

- **Specifications / 规格**: Quality specifications
- **Standards / 标准**: Quality standards
- **Measurements / 测量**: Quality metrics
- **Compliance / 合规**: Quality compliance

### 7.3 直观解释配例 / Intuitive Explanation with One Example

**Example 7.3** (质量规划即蓝图 + 一例 / Quality Planning as Blueprint with One Example)

用「蓝图」比喻：规格=质量要求、标准=基准、测量=指标、合规=达标判定。例如 $plan(P_{sw})=QualityPlan_{sw}$ 含性能、可靠性、安全；$plan(P_{constr})=QualityPlan_{constr}$ 含安全、耐久、法规符合。

## 8. Argumentation / 论证

### 8.1 Why Planning Exists / 为什么规划存在

**Argument 8.1** (Preparation Argument)

Planning exists because:

1. Quality needs preparation
2. Preparation requires planning
3. Planning provides structure
4. Structure enables achievement
