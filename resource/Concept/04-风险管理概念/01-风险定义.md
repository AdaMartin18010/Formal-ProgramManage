**English / 英文**:

Risk is an uncertain event or condition that, if it occurs, has a positive or negative effect on project objectives. This document provides comprehensive coverage of risk definitions from multiple perspectives, including formal definitions, category-theoretic perspectives, and practical applications.

- **Risk Event / 风险事件**: Uncertain event / 不确定事件
- **Risk Probability / 风险概率**: Probability of occurrence / 发生概率

## 2. Definition / 定义

### 2.1 PMBOK 7th Edition Definition / PMBOK 第7版定义

**Definition 2.1** (Risk - PMBOK 7th Edition)

Risk is an uncertain event or condition that, if it occurs, has a positive or negative effect on one or more project objectives.

**Key Components / 关键组件**:

- **Event / 事件**: What might happen
- **Probability / 概率**: Likelihood of occurrence
- **Impact / 影响**: Effect on objectives
- **Response / 应对**: Response strategy

### 2.2 ISO 31000 Standard Definition / ISO 31000 标准定义

**Definition 2.2** (Risk - ISO 31000:2018)

Risk is the effect of uncertainty on objectives.

### 2.3 Formal Mathematical Definition / 形式化数学定义

**Definition 2.3** (Risk - Formal Definition)

A risk is a tuple:

$$Risk = (Event, Probability, Impact, State, Response)$$

where:

- $Event$ is the risk event
- $Probability \in [0,1]$ is the probability
- $Impact$ is the impact on objectives
- $State$ is the risk state
- $Response$ is the response strategy

## 3. Category Theory Perspective / 范畴论视角

### 3.1 Risk as Object / 风险作为对象

**Definition 3.1** (Risk Object)

A risk $Risk \in \mathbf{Risk}$ is an object:

$$Risk = (Event, Probability, Impact, State, Response)$$

### 3.2 Risk Morphisms / 风险态射

**Definition 3.2** (Risk Management Morphism)

A risk management morphism $manage: Risk \to RiskState$:

$$manage(Risk) = RiskState$$

## 4. Properties / 性质

### 4.1 Basic Properties / 基本性质

**Property 4.1** (Risk Existence)

Risks exist:

$$\forall P \in \mathbf{Project}: \exists Risk \in Risk(P)$$

**Property 4.2** (Risk Probability)

Risks have probability:

$$\forall Risk: Probability(Risk) \in [0,1]$$

## 6. Examples / 例子

### 6.1 Technical Risk Example / 技术风险例子

**Example 6.1** (Technology Risk)

Consider technology risk:

$$Risk_{tech} = (TechFailure, 0.3, High, Identified, Mitigate)$$

with 30% probability.

### 6.2 Schedule Risk Example / 进度风险例子

**Example 6.2** (Schedule Risk)

Consider schedule risk:

$$Risk_{schedule} = (Delay, 0.5, Medium, Identified, Accept)$$

with 50% probability.

## 7. Explanations / 解释

### 7.1 Intuitive Explanation / 直观解释

**Explanation 7.1** ("Weather Forecast" Metaphor)

Risk is like weather forecast:

- **Event / 事件**: Weather condition
- **Probability / 概率**: Forecast probability
- **Impact / 影响**: Effect on plans
- **Response / 应对**: Preparation strategy

### 7.3 直观解释配例 / Intuitive Explanation with One Example

**Example 7.3** (风险即天气预报 + 一例 / Risk as Weather Forecast with One Example)

用「天气预报」比喻：事件=天气、概率=预报概率、影响=对计划的影响、应对=预案。例如 $Risk=(TechFailure, 0.3, High, Mitigate)$ 即 30% 技术失败、高影响、应对为缓解；$Risk=(Delay, 0.5, Medium, Accept)$ 即 50% 延期、中影响、接受。

## 8. Argumentation / 论证

### 8.1 Why Risk Exists / 为什么风险存在

**Argument 8.1** (Uncertainty Argument)

Risk exists because:

1. Projects have uncertainty
2. Uncertainty creates risk
3. Risk needs management
4. Management enables success
