## 1. Overview / 概述

**English / 英文**:

Business project management manages business projects using project management principles and business practices. It includes product launches, market expansion, and business value delivery. This document provides comprehensive coverage of business project management from multiple perspectives.

**中文**:

商业项目管理使用项目管理原则和商业实践管理商业项目。它包括产品发布、市场扩张和商业价值交付。本文档从多个视角全面覆盖商业项目管理。

**Key Insights / 关键洞察**:

- **Business Projects / 商业项目**: Business development projects / 商业开发项目
- **Business Value / 商业价值**: ROI, revenue, profit / ROI、收入、利润
- **Stakeholder Management / 利益相关者管理**: Managing stakeholders / 管理利益相关者
- **Business Metrics / 商业指标**: Business performance metrics / 商业绩效指标

## 2. Definition / 定义

### 2.1 PMBOK 7th Edition Definition / PMBOK 第7版定义

**Definition 2.1** (Business Project Management - PMBOK 7th)

Business project management applies project management principles to business:

$$BusinessPM: \mathbf{BusinessProject} \to \mathbf{BusinessOutcome}$$

**Key Activities / 关键活动**:

- **Planning / 规划**: Business project planning
- **Execution / 执行**: Business execution
- **Value Delivery / 价值交付**: Delivering business value
- **Stakeholder Management / 利益相关者管理**: Managing stakeholders

### 2.2 Business Management Definition / 商业管理定义

**Definition 2.2** (Business Project Management - Business Management)

Business project management manages business initiatives:

$$BusinessPM = (Planning, Execution, ValueDelivery, StakeholderManagement)$$

## 3. Category Theory Perspective / 范畴论视角

### 3.1 Business Projects as Objects / 商业项目作为对象

**Definition 3.1** (Business Project Object)

Business projects are objects in $\mathbf{Business}$:

$$P_{biz} \in \mathbf{Business}$$

with business-specific properties.

### 3.2 Business Processes as Morphisms / 商业过程作为态射

**Definition 3.2** (Business Process Morphism)

Business processes are morphisms:

$$process: P_1 \to P_2$$

transforming business projects.

---

## 4. Properties / 性质

### 4.1 Basic Properties / 基本性质

**Property 4.1** (Business Value)

Business projects have value:

$$\forall P_{biz}: Value(P_{biz}) \geq 0$$

**Property 4.2** (Business ROI)

Business projects have ROI:

$$\forall P_{biz}: ROI(P_{biz}) \in \mathbb{R}$$

## 7. Explanations / 解释

### 7.1 Intuitive Explanation / 直观解释

**Explanation 7.1** ("Business Initiative" Metaphor)

Business project management is like managing business initiatives:

- **Planning / 规划**: Planning business initiatives / 规划商业计划
- **Execution / 执行**: Executing initiatives / 执行计划
- **Value Delivery / 价值交付**: Delivering value / 交付价值
- **Stakeholder Management / 利益相关者管理**: Managing stakeholders / 管理利益相关者

### 7.3 直观解释配例 / Intuitive Explanation with One Example

**Example 7.3** (Business Initiative 配例)

按 7.1「Business Initiative」比喻：规划如设定目标与里程碑、执行为资源投入与进度管控、价值交付与利益相关者管理如 ROI 与沟通。例如产品发布 $P_{launch} = (NewProduct, 3Months, MarketingTeam, Budget, ExpectedROI)$：从规划到执行的态射对应市场验证与迭代，$Resource: \mathbf{Business} \to \mathbf{Resource}$ 体现预算与团队在商业项目中的约束与配置。
