## 1. Overview / 概述

**English / 英文**:

Healthcare project management manages healthcare projects using project management principles and healthcare industry practices. It includes clinical care, medical research, regulatory compliance, and quality assurance. This document provides comprehensive coverage of healthcare project management from multiple perspectives.

**中文**:

医疗项目管理使用项目管理原则和医疗行业实践管理医疗项目。它包括临床护理、医学研究、监管合规和质量保证。本文档从多个视角全面覆盖医疗项目管理。

**Key Insights / 关键洞察**:

- **Healthcare Projects / 医疗项目**: Clinical care and medical research projects / 临床护理和医学研究项目
- **Clinical Processes / 临床过程**: Clinical care processes / 临床护理过程
- **Research Processes / 研究过程**: Medical research processes / 医学研究过程
- **Regulatory Compliance / 监管合规**: Healthcare regulatory compliance / 医疗监管合规

## 2. Definition / 定义

### 2.1 PMBOK 7th Edition Definition / PMBOK 第7版定义

**Definition 2.1** (Healthcare Project Management - PMBOK 7th)

Healthcare project management applies project management principles to healthcare:

$$HealthcarePM: \mathbf{HealthcareProject} \to \mathbf{HealthcareOutcome}$$

**Key Activities / 关键活动**:

- **Clinical Care / 临床护理**: Providing clinical care
- **Research / 研究**: Conducting medical research
- **Regulatory Compliance / 监管合规**: Complying with regulations
- **Quality Assurance / 质量保证**: Ensuring quality

### 2.2 Healthcare Industry Definition / 医疗行业定义

**Definition 2.2** (Healthcare Project Management - Healthcare Industry)

Healthcare project management manages healthcare lifecycle:

$$HealthcarePM = (ClinicalCare, Research, RegulatoryCompliance, QualityAssurance)$$

## 7. Explanations / 解释

### 7.1 Intuitive Explanation / 直观解释

**Explanation 7.1** ("Healthcare Delivery" Metaphor)

Healthcare project management is like healthcare delivery:

- **Clinical Care / 临床护理**: Providing patient care / 提供患者护理
- **Research / 研究**: Conducting research / 进行研究
- **Compliance / 合规**: Ensuring compliance / 确保合规
- **Quality / 质量**: Ensuring quality / 确保质量

### 7.3 直观解释配例 / Intuitive Explanation with One Example

**Example 7.3** (Healthcare Delivery 配例)

按 7.1「Healthcare Delivery」比喻：临床护理如患者路径管理、研究如试验设计执行、合规与质量如 GCP/监管与质控。例如临床试验 $P_{trial} = (TrialDesign, PatientRecruitment, DataCollection, RegulatoryCompliance)$：从 TrialDesign 到 DataCollection 的态射对应 方案→入组→采集 的临床路径，$Quality: \mathbf{Healthcare} \to \mathbf{Quality}$ 与合规要求体现 7.1 中「合规」「质量」在形式框架中的位置。
