## 1. Overview / 概述

**English / 英文**:

Business project management application framework applies business-specific project management practices to business projects. It captures strategy management, operations management, and business change management. This document provides comprehensive coverage of business project management application framework from multiple perspectives.

**中文**:

商业项目管理应用框架将商业特定的项目管理实践应用于商业项目。它捕捉战略管理、运营管理和商业变更管理。本文档从多个视角全面覆盖商业项目管理应用框架。

**Key Insights / 关键洞察**:

- **Strategy Management / 战略管理**: Business strategy management / 商业战略管理
- **Operations Management / 运营管理**: Business operations management / 商业运营管理
- **Change Management / 变更管理**: Business change management / 商业变更管理

## 2. Definition / 定义

### 2.1 Business PM Application Definition / 商业项目管理应用定义

**Definition 2.1** (Business Project Management Application)

A business project management application applies:

$$BusinessPMApp: \mathbf{BusinessProject} \times \mathbf{BusinessPractices} \to \mathbf{Result}$$

business practices to business projects.

### 2.2 Framework Definition / 框架定义

**Definition 2.2** (Business PM Application Framework)

The business PM application framework is:

$$BusinessPMFramework = \{Strategy, Operations, Change, Value\}$$

with strategy, operations, change, and value.

---

## 3. Category Theory Perspective / 范畴论视角

### 3.1 Business PM Application as Functor / 商业项目管理应用作为函子

**Definition 3.1** (Business PM Application Functor)

Business PM application is a functor:

$$BusinessPMApp: \mathbf{BusinessProject} \times \mathbf{BusinessPractices} \to \mathbf{Result}$$

applying business practices.

### 3.2 Framework Properties / 框架性质

**Axiom 3.1** (Business PM Application Value)

Business PM applications deliver value:

$$\forall BusinessPMApp: Value(BusinessPMApp(P, Practices))$$

---

## 4. Properties / 性质

### 4.1 Basic Properties / 基本性质

**Property 4.1** (Strategy Application)

Business PM applications support strategy:

$$\forall BusinessPMApp: Supports(Strategy)$$

**Property 4.2** (Operations Application)

Business PM applications support operations:

$$\forall BusinessPMApp: Supports(Operations)$$

## 5. Relations / 关系

### 5.1 Relations to Other Concepts / 与其他概念的关系

**Relation 5.1** (Business PM Application → Business Project)

Business PM applications apply to business projects:

$$BusinessProject: \mathbf{BusinessPMApp} \to \mathbf{BusinessProject}$$

**Relation 5.2** (Business PM Application → Business Practices)

Business PM applications use business practices:

$$BusinessPractices: \mathbf{BusinessPMApp} \to \mathbf{BusinessPractices}$$

## 8. Argumentation / 论证

### 8.1 Why Business PM Applications Exist / 为什么商业项目管理应用存在

**Argument 8.1** (Business Argument)

Business PM applications exist because:

1. Business projects have specific needs
2. Needs require business practices
3. Practices need application
4. Application enables value
